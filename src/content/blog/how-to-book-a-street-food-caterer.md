Street food catering has become a popular choice for weddings, birthdays and corporate events. It's relaxed, it's generous and guests can choose what they eat. Here's what to think about before you book.

## 1. Lock in your date early

Good caterers book up, especially for summer weekends. Get in touch as soon as you have a date and a venue, even if the guest list isn't final.

## 2. Know your numbers (roughly)

You don't need an exact headcount on day one, but a realistic range helps a caterer plan staffing and stock. Final numbers are normally confirmed closer to the date.

## 3. Think about dietary needs

Ask about vegetarian, vegan and allergen options, and tell your caterer about any guest requirements up front. A good street food operator will talk you through the menu honestly.

## 4. Check the venue

Access for a catering unit, space to park and set up, and availability of power and water can all matter. Some venues have rules about outside caterers, so check before you commit.

## 5. Ask about insurance and hygiene

A professional caterer should be happy to talk about food hygiene registration, allergen procedures and public liability insurance.

## 6. Agree the format

Will guests queue at the hatch, or will you want a set service time? Will it be the main meal, or an evening feast after the ceremony? Decide early and the rest falls into place.
