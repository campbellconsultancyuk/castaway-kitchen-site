It's nearly that time — **2000trees is back from 8th–11th July**, and Castaway Kitchen will be right there trading across the weekend. If you've got tickets, we'll see you on site. If you're still deciding, consider this your excuse.

We're proud to be a returning trader again this year, dishing up alongside some brilliant food alongside us on the lineup. Good company, good food, good festival — exactly how we like it.

## What We're Serving

Traditional gyros, done properly. Fresh, packed with flavour, and built to fuel a full day of music without slowing you down. If you know Castaway, you know what to expect — if you don't, come find out.

## Why 2000trees

There's a reason we keep coming back. It's a proper independent festival with a strong sense of community, and it's always one of the best weekends on our calendar. Good crowd, great atmosphere, and food traders who genuinely care about what they're serving — us included.

## Find Us On Site

Keep an eye on our socials in the lead-up for exactly where we'll be pitched once the site map's confirmed — we'll post as soon as we know. Until then: 8th–11th July, Cheltenham, and we'll have the grills going all weekend.

See you there.
