Every season needs a proper last night. For Castaway Kitchen in 2026, that's the **Cheltenham Literature Festival, 9th–18th October**, the last official event on our calendar and a fitting one: it's right on our home patch.

## A very different crowd

We've spent the year feeding people at music festivals, from indie fields to castle grounds. The Literature Festival is a different beast. It runs for ten days, with more than 500 events, and around a third of the programme is free to attend. Expect authors, comedians, politicians, chefs and a lot of people who have queued for a good seat and would very much like some lunch.

We think that's a great crowd to end on.

## What to expect from us

Same Castaway you know, just with a few more tote bags around. Our [gyros-led menu](/menu/) is what we're known for, with the Korean and poke bowls alongside for anyone after something lighter between sessions.

## Find us

We'll post exactly where we're pitched on our socials as soon as it's confirmed, so follow us on Instagram and Facebook (@castawaykitchen) to avoid wandering around Cheltenham with a rumbling stomach.

You can also follow the whole season on our [festival map](/festivals/), where the final pin has just gone in.

## What's next?

The unit doesn't stop when the season does. If you're planning a party, a wedding or a corporate event, have a look at our [private catering](/private-catering/) and get in touch. Winter dates go quicker than you'd think.

See you in Cheltenham.
